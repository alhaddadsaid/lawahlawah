# Reelway Browser privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Reelway Browser ("Reelway") is a web browser for watching videos. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## What Reelway stores

Websites you add, bookmarks, pages you visit, viewing progress and your settings. This data stays on your device. Reelway has no user accounts and does not upload this data to us.

## Websites you visit

Reelway loads websites and their scripts like any browser. Websites and their video providers receive normal network request information, including your IP address, and may use cookies under their own privacy policies. When a page plays a video, Reelway can play that video in its own player; the request goes directly from your device to the website's video server. You can clear website data, history and all app data in Settings.

## Advertising

Reelway is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (banners on Reelway's own screens, full-screen ads between some episodes and when you return to the app). Google may use device identifiers and cookies to show and measure ads.

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Reelway asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
- On iPhone, Reelway asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.

Google's use of data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## Ad blocking list

The browser blocks known ad networks using EasyList (https://easylist.to), under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license. This happens on your device.

## Children

Reelway is not directed at children under 13.
