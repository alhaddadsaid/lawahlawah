## Full description

Reelway is a lightweight video browser for watching videos and browsing your favorite websites with fewer interruptions.

Open video sites, stop pop-ups and surprise redirects, and watch in a simple dark interface.

Key features:

• Video browsing
Open your favorite video and entertainment websites in one fast browser.

• Fewer pop-ups and redirects
Windows and jumps you did not tap are stopped, so you decide what opens. Choose Balanced, Strict or Off, and allow pop-ups on the sites you trust.

• Clean video player
Videos on a page can play in a simple full-screen player with playback speed, resume, and the next video playing automatically.

• Popular sites
Quick access to your favorite websites from the start page. Add your own.

• Tabs, bookmarks and history
Open several tabs, save pages and find them again.

• Fast and lightweight
A clean dark interface made for smooth browsing and watching.

Why Reelway?

• Made for video websites
• Fewer pop-ups and surprise redirects
• Clean player with speed and resume
• Simple tabs, bookmarks and history
• Dark interface for comfortable viewing

Note:
Reelway is a web browser and does not host or provide any content. Users are responsible for the websites and content they choose to access.
